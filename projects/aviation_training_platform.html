Aviation Training Platform

Senior Software Engineer at AVIAR

Overview

I work as a Senior Software Engineer at AVIAR, an Amsterdam company founded in 2019 that builds VR training for the aviation industry. The team combines XR developers with aviation experts, and the goal is to make VR a standard way to train staff in air transportation.

Everything below comes from the company’s public website. Product internals are covered by an NDA, so I don’t describe them here.

Video from the official AVIAR website.

What the platform does

According to aviar.nl, the platform includes:

  • VR Training Hub — a library of training modules that runs on standalone VR headsets.
  • Training Station Software — lets a trainer prepare, run and monitor sessions on several headsets from a PC, laptop or tablet on the local network, without an internet connection.
  • Driver Training — driving accessories connect directly to the headset, so no PC is needed.
  • All-In-One VR Training Box — hardware, software, accounts and device management delivered as a subscription, with remote updates and trainer onboarding.
  • Custom VR Training — modules built to a client’s own procedures.

Training areas

The website lists ground operations and cabin crew training, with MRO and airport security training announced as coming soon. Ground operations modules include pre-arrival checks, aircraft marshalling, GSE connection, pre-departure checks, cargo door operations and pushback with a towbar.

Clients

Public clients shown on the website include Lufthansa, Swissport, DHL, dnata, Worldwide Flight Services, Schiphol, Cebu Pacific, Asiana Airlines and Celebi.

My role

I develop training scenarios and core features of the platform for these clients. For technical details, I’m happy to talk in person within what the NDA allows.
